Method

Gross pay is the sum of base pay, incentives and tips across your confirmed trips. Platform deductions are subtracted to get net payout. Your own costs (fuel and maintenance per km, plus any fixed daily cost) are subtracted from net payout to get net earnings. Net earnings is divided by engaged hours (the time you were actually on a trip) and, separately, by logged-in hours (your total time online) to give two different hourly rates, since they measure different things. Net payout is also divided by total distance to give pay per km. Deduction share is platform deductions divided by gross pay.
